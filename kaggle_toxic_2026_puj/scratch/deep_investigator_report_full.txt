### Mathematical Proof of Equivalence:
For any classifier with calibrated logit $z = \text{logit}(P_{\text{blend}})$, applying temperature scaling $P_{\text{cal}} = \sigma(z / T)$ and comparing against threshold $\tau$ is strictly isomorphic to thresholding $P_{\text{blend}}$ directly:
$$P_{\text{cal}} \ge \tau \iff \frac{z}{T} \ge \text{logit}(\tau) \iff z \ge T \cdot \text{logit}(\tau) \iff P_{\text{blend}} \ge \sigma(T \cdot \text{logit}(\tau))$$
Temperature scaling is a strictly monotonic function. It **does not alter the ranking of samples**, cannot change the ROC curve, cannot change the PR curve, and cannot improve separability.
